How the coordinates work

South is entered as a positive number of degrees. The map is treated as linear in degrees between its grid lines, so a site's position on the image is:

x = 114.5 + (E − 18) · 687.5 / 14     (pixels, image 958 wide)
y =  58.0 + (S − 22) · 588.5 / 12     (pixels, image 768 high)

Accepted range: E 16.4 – 33.0, S 22.0 – 34.9

When a municipality is selected, vb,0 is its value from the municipality table. Otherwise the page reads the zone colour under the site. Where two zones meet, it uses the higher value and shows a warning. If there is no zone under the site, it falls back to 36 m/s.

Municipality points are centroids. Large or irregular municipalities can have a centroid that falls in a neighbouring zone, so the table value and the colour under the point can differ. For 14 of the 235 municipalities they do.
